FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Franco financial advice?

No. Franco is a tool that helps you track and model your own finances. It doesn't recommend specific investments or give personal financial advice. For advice tailored to your situation, talk to a qualified financial adviser.

Where does my data go?

Your financial data stays on your device. Franco doesn't run analytics, doesn't share data with third parties, and doesn't sell anything about you. If you turn on iCloud sync, your data is encrypted in your private iCloud account — Apple's standard end-to-end encryption applies. Franco never sees it.

Market prices are fetched from public APIs (Yahoo Finance, CoinGecko), but no personal data is sent in those requests.

Can I import my brokerage data?

Franco supports importing trades via CSV from most major Australian brokers, plus the iOS share extension lets you grab statements directly from PDF or email. Manual entry is also fully supported for any unusual cases.

Will Franco file my tax return?

No — Franco produces a tax PDF with every figure you need to complete your own ATO myTax return (or hand to your accountant). The PDF maps each amount to the correct myTax label (T, U, P, etc.) so you can copy the numbers across in a few minutes.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Franco is iPhone and iPad only at launch. An Android version may come in the future depending on demand.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

Your data stays on your device — it's not deleted. You'll lose access to the app's features after your subscription ends, but if you resubscribe later, everything's still there.

Why a subscription instead of a one-off purchase?

Honest answer: Franco needs ongoing development to keep up with ATO changes, tax law updates, broker changes, and iOS updates. A subscription pays for that ongoing work, instead of selling out to advertisers or selling your data. It's the model that lets a small indie app stay alive without compromising your privacy.
