Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 May 2026

Franco stores all your financial data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share your personal or financial information with any third parties, except for the limited external requests described below.

Data stored on your device

All data you enter into Franco — including share trades, dividends, property details, mortgage balances, rental income, property expenses, superannuation balances, crypto transactions, and your FIRE goal — is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. This data is never transmitted to our servers.

Share and crypto price data

To display live prices, Franco makes outbound requests to Yahoo Finance (ASX and international share prices) and CoinGecko (cryptocurrency prices). These requests contain only ticker symbols or coin identifiers — no personal data, account details, or portfolio information is included. Franco has no control over the privacy practices of these third-party providers.

Address autocomplete

When adding a property, Franco may query an address search service to suggest property addresses as you type. Only the partial address text you have typed is sent in these requests — no other personal or financial data is transmitted. If you prefer not to use autocomplete, you can type your address directly without selecting a suggestion.

Backup files

Franco can export a backup file containing all your portfolio data in JSON format. This file is saved to your device and shared only through the iOS share sheet at your explicit direction — for example, to save to Files, email to yourself, or store in iCloud Drive. Franco does not automatically upload backup files anywhere. You are responsible for the security of any backup files you create and share.

CSV imports

When you import a broker or exchange CSV file, that file is read locally on your device and is not transmitted to any server. Imported transaction data is stored alongside your other data on-device.

Analytics and crash reporting

Franco does not use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, or behavioural tracking. We have no visibility into how you use the app, what data you enter, or what features you access.

Data deletion

You can delete all Franco data at any time using the Wipe All Data option in Settings, or by uninstalling the app. All locally stored data will be permanently removed. Backup files you have saved outside the app (for example in Files or iCloud Drive) are not affected and must be deleted manually.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at admin@francoapp.com.au.
